How the breach check stays private

Your password is checked privately in four simple steps.

1

The browser hashes it

The SHA-1 digest of your password is computed locally with the Web Crypto API. The password itself never touches the network.

2

Five characters are sent

Only the first five characters of the digest — 5BAA6 — are requested from the range API. That prefix is shared by roughly a million different passwords.

3

The server returns a list

It replies with every suffix in that range plus a count. It has no way to tell which entry you were looking for.

4

The match happens on your device

Your browser compares its own suffix against the list and reports the result. The server never learns it.

Why this beats a complexity meter

NIST SP 800-63B recommends length and screening against known-bad passwords over composition rules. Here is what that looks like in practice.
